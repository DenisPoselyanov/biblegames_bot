> **What changed with the quality gate (layers 0–1).** The WS10 audit reported 1 088 items (1.2%) needing repair. That number was wrong in the reassuring direction: the WS3 checks never looked at the reference at all. A hand-read of the bank showed misfiled questions (the Pentecost of Acts 2 under Pentateuch → Leviticus; Philip the deacon under "the twelve apostles → Philip") and outright hallucinations ("Які дві перші заповіді написані в книзі Буття?", "Що вбачав Ілля над Галілейським озером?" citing Matthew 3). Layer 1 now checks every reference against the theme's books (`server/domains/content/themeCanon.ts`), which moves 12 789 items into `needs_repair`.
>
> `theme_canon_mismatch` is **triage, not a verdict**: the bucket mixes legitimate cross-references, questions filed under the wrong theme, and inventions. Telling them apart needs the verse text and judgment — that is layer 2 (the AI reviewer grounded in the fetched verse), then a human in Studio. Nothing here is deleted or hidden.
