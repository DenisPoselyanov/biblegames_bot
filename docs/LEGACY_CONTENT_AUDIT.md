> ⚠️ **Position bias.** The correct answer sits at **B in more than half** of all questions and almost never at D. A player who always picks B beats chance by a wide margin. The game does not compensate today: `src/data/questions.ts` shuffles *question order*, never the options. This is a bank-wide generation artefact, not a per-question defect — the fix is shuffling options (render-time, remapping `correctIndex`) or a repair pass; WS9's live `question_option_picks` will show whether players already exploit it. Not changed in WS10 — it alters gameplay, so it needs an owner decision.
